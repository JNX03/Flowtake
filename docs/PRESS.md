### Two-paragraph (for Dev.to intro, press inquiries)

Flowtake is a free, open-source desktop screen recorder that turns ordinary screen captures into polished product demos. It automatically adds cinematic zoom and pan animations that follow your cursor, smooths cursor motion with velocity-based inertia, and ships with a full built-in timeline editor for clips, effects, overlays, subtitles, and masks.

Unlike Loom, Flowtake runs entirely locally — no cloud, no account, no upload. Unlike Screen Studio, it's free, open-source (MIT), and cross-platform. Unlike OBS, it focuses on polish over raw capture. Built with Tauri v2 (Rust) for a small native footprint and fast startup. Perfect for developers, educators, and product marketers who want Screen Studio–quality output without the price tag.

### Long (3 paragraphs — for features, comparison articles)

Flowtake is an open-source screen recorder designed around a single idea: your recordings should look like a polished product demo with zero editing effort. Start a recording, click around your app, stop. Flowtake automatically generates smooth zoom transitions around your cursor, pan animations that anticipate your movements, and velocity-based cursor inertia with motion blur scaled by speed — the kind of cinematic polish that usually takes hours of manual keyframing.

Beyond auto-animation, Flowtake ships with a full timeline editor built on Pixi.js: clip trimming, overlays, audio tracks, subtitles with speech recognition, masks and blur for redacting sensitive areas, custom backgrounds, and configurable intro/outro transitions. There's even a built-in teleprompter with speech-sync — unique among screen recorders — and a preset system for saving recording configurations. Exports run through a bundled FFmpeg sidecar with configurable quality, format, and encoder settings.

The app is built with Tauri v2 and Rust for a lightweight, native experience (unlike Electron-based alternatives), licensed under MIT, and runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux. It's free forever — no subscription, no cloud requirement, no account. Everything stays on your machine. Flowtake has been in active development for over two years with 367+ commits, and v1.4.2 is available now for download from GitHub Releases.

## Key differentiators

- **Auto-zoom animations** — intelligent zoom effects that follow cursor and focus areas
- **Pan animations** — smooth camera panning with velocity-based camera leading for natural follow
- **Cursor inertia + motion blur** — adaptive smoothing with blur scaled by speed
- **Built-in timeline editor** — clips, effects, overlays, audio tracks, subtitles, masks
- **Teleprompter** — unique feature with speech recognition sync
- **Local-first** — no cloud, no account, no upload required
- **Cross-platform** — Windows, macOS, Linux (Windows stable; macOS/Linux in dev preview)
- **Open source** — MIT license, fork-friendly
- **Free** — no subscription, no upgrade, no lock-in
- **Tauri-native** — ~50-100MB footprint vs 300MB+ Electron apps
