# Flowtake Outreach Drafts

These drafts are ready for review, editing, and posting. Public submission still
needs account access and explicit approval from the project owner.

## Hacker News

Title:

```text
Show HN: Flowtake - open-source screen recorder with automatic zooms
```

Body:

```text
Hi HN, I built Flowtake, a free and open-source desktop screen recorder that turns raw screen recordings into polished demos with automatic zooms, pans, cursor smoothing, captions, masks/redaction, and export presets.

The short version: I wanted Screen Studio-style output, but local, hackable, and available beyond macOS.

It is built with Tauri v2, Rust, React, Pixi.js, and FFmpeg. Windows is the most stable path today; macOS and Linux builds exist, but I am treating them as developer-preview quality until more people try them on real machines.

GitHub: https://github.com/JNX03/Flowtake
Demo: https://github.com/JNX03/Flowtake/blob/main/docs/demos/flowtake-demo.mp4

I would love feedback on:
- whether the auto-zoom behavior feels natural
- missing export presets
- platform-specific recording bugs
- what would make this good enough for your product demos or tutorials
```
